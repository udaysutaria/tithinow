Privacy Policy for TithiNow

Effective Date: October 18, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 18, 2026

Privacy-First Summary: TithiNow is a 100% offline-first application. We do not collect, transmit, track, or sell your personal data. All astronomical calculations occur locally on your device without user registration.

1. Overview

Welcome to TithiNow ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to complete transparency. This Privacy Policy details how our mobile and desktop application handles information when you use our service.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute data minimization. TithiNow does NOT collect:

  • Personal identifiers (names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts).
  • GPS or precise real-time background location data.
  • Advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA) or biometric information.
  • Payment or financial details.
  • Cookies, browsing analytics, or device fingerprinting.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide accurate Vedic Panchang timings and remember your settings, TithiNow stores minimal configuration data locally on your device via an offline SQLite database:

  • Selected City: The city name and latitude/longitude selected from our offline database of 7,100+ Indian cities are saved locally to compute daily sunrise, sunset, and Panchang periods.
  • Language Preference: Your selected language (English, Hindi, or Gujarati) is saved locally.
  • Alarms & Reminders: The alarms you schedule for Tithis, Choghadiya, or Muhurats are stored locally and scheduled via your device's native alarm manager.

None of this data is ever transmitted to an external server.

4. Device Permissions Requested

TithiNow requests only the technical permissions necessary to operate its core alarm and timing features:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To notify you when a scheduled Muhurta or Tithi alarm triggers.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: To ensure auspicious timing alerts ring at the exact minute requested.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Temporarily used to play the alarm tone and present the full-screen alarm interface.
  • Network Access: Used exclusively when you explicitly tap an external link (such as our website or GitHub source code) to open it in your device's default web browser.

5. Open Source & Third-Party Code

TithiNow is free and open-source software licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0).

  • Planetary computations are powered by the Swiss Ephemeris library (developed by Astrodienst AG), running as embedded code locally on your phone with zero network calls.
  • Version 1.0 of the application contains no third-party tracking SDKs (no Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or Firebase Analytics) and no third-party advertisements.

6. Children's Privacy

TithiNow does not address children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for all audiences.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all data remains exclusively on your phone, you have total control:

  • You can clear all stored preferences at any time through your device settings under App Info > Clear Storage / Cache.
  • Uninstalling the app completely and permanently purges all local databases and settings.
